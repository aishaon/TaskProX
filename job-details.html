Install BrightApp & submit a written review

by BrightApp Inc. · 🌍 Worldwide

Job description

BrightApp Inc. is launching a new productivity app and needs genuine first-impression reviews from real users. You'll download the app, spend a few minutes exploring its core features, and share an honest written review on the app store. This task takes about 15 minutes and is a great fit for workers comfortable writing a few thoughtful sentences in English.

Steps to complete

  1. 1Download BrightApp from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. 2Create an account and explore the app for at least 5 minutes.
  3. 3Leave a genuine written review (minimum 40 words) rating it 4 stars or higher.
  4. 4Take a screenshot of your published review.
  5. 5Submit the screenshot and your review text as proof below.

Reviews must reflect genuine use of the app. Copy-pasted or generic reviews will be rejected and may result in account suspension.

What you need to submit

  • A clear screenshot showing your review live on the app store, including your username and star rating.
  • The full text of your review pasted into the proof field.
  • Your review must be at least 40 words and rate the app 4 stars or higher.
  • Do not submit reviews copied from other sources — this will be flagged by our AI fraud detection system.

Review timeline

Submissions are typically reviewed within 6–12 hours. You'll be notified by email once your proof is approved and the reward is added to your balance.
